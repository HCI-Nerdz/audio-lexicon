= Samples

== Generated (offline)

`samples/curated/tone-bed.wav` is generated by `pnpm generate:tone` and is released as **CC0-1.0** by HCI Nerdz.

== Curated music (default audition set)

`pnpm fetch:curated` downloads pinned **CC0 / public-domain** tracks from the Internet Archive into `samples/curated/`:

* *Catch that Wave* / *Lemon Yellow* — pop-like / bright synth (AI AM Pop)
* *FYAH Dubstep* — bass and drop stress test (kosmik pack)
* *Voice of Jah People* — reggae/dnb vocal + transient material (Paper Planes–*role* stand-in)
* *Beethoven Symphony No. 5, I* — Musopen digital classical master

Commercial recordings (**Skrillex**, **M.I.A. Paper Planes**, major-label pop, etc.) are **not** bundled.
Appearing on SoundCloud or a free stream does not grant redistribution rights.

== Online browser

The web UI sample pane lists curated metadata and can open Internet Archive searches for more CC0/PD audio.
`pnpm fetch:samples` optionally caches additional IA items under `samples/cache/` (not committed).

Never ship scraped tracks without license metadata in the catalog.
